    <section class="section laws-section" id="laws">
      <div class="container">
        <div class="section-title-wrap zoom-reveal">
          <span class="kicker">FIRST PRINCIPLES</span>
          <h2 class="section-heading">Built on the 4 laws of reliable backend engineering</h2>
        </div>

        <div class="laws-slider-track">
          <!-- Law 1 -->
          <div class="law-card zoom-reveal interactive-hover-zoom" data-delay="0" data-cursor="view">
            <div class="law-num-bubble">1.</div>
            <h3 class="law-title">Make it <br><em>deterministic.</em></h3>
            <p class="law-desc">
              Never trust client data. Validate every payload with strict server-side serializers, boundary checks, and explicit HTTP contracts.
            </p>
            <div class="law-tag">Django REST Framework</div>
          </div>

          <!-- Law 2 -->
          <div class="law-card zoom-reveal interactive-hover-zoom" data-delay="120" data-cursor="view">
            <div class="law-num-bubble">2.</div>
            <h3 class="law-title">Make it <br><em>transactional.</em></h3>
            <p class="law-desc">
              Design normalized schemas with database-level constraints (<code class="code-pill">unique_together</code>) so race conditions physically cannot corrupt state.
            </p>
            <div class="law-tag">PostgreSQL / MySQL</div>
          </div>

          <!-- Law 3 -->
          <div class="law-card zoom-reveal interactive-hover-zoom" data-delay="240" data-cursor="view">
            <div class="law-num-bubble">3.</div>
            <h3 class="law-title">Make it <br><em>observable.</em></h3>
            <p class="law-desc">
              Understand what happens on the wire. Sniff Layer 3/4 network traffic with Scapy and Wireshark to verify TCP flags and isolate latency.
            </p>
            <div class="law-tag">Scapy & Wireshark</div>
          </div>

          <!-- Law 4 -->
          <div class="law-card zoom-reveal interactive-hover-zoom" data-delay="360" data-cursor="view">
            <div class="law-num-bubble">4.</div>
            <h3 class="law-title">Make it <br><em>grounded.</em></h3>
            <p class="law-desc">
              AI without verification is a gamble. Ground foundation models with private database context using vector search to guarantee zero hallucinations.
            </p>
            <div class="law-tag">OCI GenAI & pgvector</div>
          </div>
        </div>

            <div class="box-tabs" data-tabs="rag">
              <div class="tab-pills">
                <button class="tab-pill active" data-tab="story" data-cursor="link">Why I Built This</button>

                <p>Dense administrative circulars and syllabi are unsearchable with basic keywords, while naive LLMs hallucinate exam dates.                 <div class="schema-card">

    <section class="section notes-section" id="notes">
      <div class="container">
        <div class="section-title-wrap zoom-reveal">
          <span class="kicker">PRACTICE & REFLECTION</span>
          <h2 class="section-heading">How I think about backend systems</h2>
        </div>

        <div class="notes-grid">
          <div class="note-item zoom-reveal interactive-hover-zoom" data-cursor="read">
            <div class="note-num">01</div>
            <h3 class="note-header">Database schemas outlive code</h3>
            <p>You can refactor ugly Python code in an afternoon. But migrating a malformed relational database schema with millions of rows in production is dangerous. I always spend extra time normalizing entities, choosing strict foreign keys, and planning indexes.</p>
          </div>

          <div class="note-item zoom-reveal interactive-hover-zoom" data-cursor="read">
            <div class="note-num">02</div>
            <h3 class="note-header">Beware the silent N+1 query</h3>
            <p>Django's ORM is wonderful until you iterate over a related field in a template and quietly trigger 300 database queries for 300 rows. Learning to proactively apply <code class="code-pill">select_related</code> and <code class="code-pill">prefetch_related</code> fundamentally changed how I write endpoints.</p>
          </div>

          <div class="note-item zoom-reveal interactive-hover-zoom" data-cursor="read">
            <div class="note-num">03</div>
            <h3 class="note-header">AI is a component, not magic</h3>
            <p>Having earned dual Oracle OCI certifications in Generative AI and Data Science, my principle is simple: LLMs are probabilistic text predictors. Unless you ground them with verified vector context and validate their output with server schemas, you are gambling with reliability.</p>
          </div>

          <div class="note-item zoom-reveal interactive-hover-zoom" data-cursor="read">
            <div class="note-num">04</div>
            <h3 class="note-header">Understand the wire</h3>
            <p>Sniffing packets with Scapy demystified the network for me. Understanding how TCP SYN, ACK, and FIN flags look in raw hexadecimal makes you a much better debugger when an API call times out across production gateways.</p>
          </div>
        </div>
